Chitkara University

Chitkara University is a well-known private university in Punjab, India, recognized for its focus on innovation, research, and industry-oriented education.

About the University

Established with a vision to provide world-class education, Chitkara University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, management, design, law, pharmacy, and other professional fields.

Campus and Learning

The university is known for its modern campus, strong academic infrastructure, experienced faculty, and collaborations with industry partners. It encourages students to develop both technical skills and leadership qualities.

Popular Programs

  • B.Tech
  • BBA
  • BCA
  • MBA
  • Pharmacy and Health Sciences
  • Design and Architecture

Why Students Choose It

Chitkara University focuses on practical learning, internships, placements, and entrepreneurship, helping students prepare for successful careers in a competitive world.

Location

It is located in Punjab, India, and is known for creating a vibrant student environment with opportunities for academic and personal growth.
